The film

S6–S7 · 10 min

Source
Museum Berlin-Karlshorst, KAPFILM, 7:36
Working cut
04:14–04:44 — the signing. 901 frames at 30 fps
Frame
transfer is 1280×800; picture is 1068×800 at x=106. Pillarboxed; the cut is de-pillarboxed by crop=1068:800:106:0
Audio
−91 dB mean and max end to end, every sample in one histogram bin. Dropped from the working cut
Colour
the scan carries a constant warm offset, a few levels more red than green throughout
Also present
the museum's own burned-in trilingual captions elsewhere in the reel, absent from this stretch
Discussion
  1. The crop, the transfer, the tint and the museum's captions all arrived as decisions someone else made. Does that make your changes more defensible, or less?
  2. This stretch was chosen partly because the museum's captions are absent from it. Is choosing the convenient thirty seconds already an edit?
  3. Nothing in this film is signed — no cameraman, no editor, no scanning technician. What can you honestly say about provenance?

Four levels

S8 · 5 min

LevelOperationModelCost
1 — quotes text composited over the frame none CPU

3 — upscale 1068×800 → 2136×1600 4x_foolhardy_Remacri (ESRGAN) 3.66 s / frame

All four ran on a local ComfyUI install, RTX 3090 Ti, 24 GB.

1 — quotes

S9–S10 · 15 min

Three quotes over the working cut

Model
none
Render
ffmpeg, libass filter, one pass over 30 s. Seconds, on CPU
Graph version
VHS_LoadVideo → DrawText+ → VHS_VideoCombine, one run per quote, ComfyUI local
Text source
transcribed by hand from the signed English copy of the Act of Military Surrender. Not read off the footage
Done to the archive
nothing. Pixels unmodified; text composited on top
Caption band
measured 14–65 of 255 across the cut, so white text with a shadow stays legible without a plate
Discussion
  1. A caption is a verbatim quote from a real document. Does putting it over a different moment of the film make it a lie? Where does timing stop being editing and start being a claim?
  2. The reel carries the museum's own burned-in captions. If yours contradicts theirs on the same frame, whose survives?
  3. Does a quote belong on screen if you cannot also show where it came from?

2 — sound

S12–S15 · 15 min

The working cut with four generated layers mixed under it

Model
Stable Audio Open 1.0, open weights, with T5-base as a separate text encoder
Where
ComfyUI local, RTX 3090 Ti
Sampler
50 steps, cfg 4.98, dpmpp_3m_sde_gpu, exponential, denoise 1.0
Generation time
≈6 s of GPU per layer, independent of requested length
Done to the archive
picture untouched. The source audio track was discarded; all four layers are generated from text
Mix
ffmpeg — atrim, adelay, per-layer gain, amix normalize=0, loudnorm I=-20 TP=-2 LRA=11

The four layers

LayerSecSeedPrompt
bed32550919Ambience of a crowded hall: many men murmuring quietly at a distance, low indistinct voices, occasional cough and chair creak, reverberant, no music.
paper14220805A single sheet of stiff dry paper lifted from a wooden table, held, turned over, and set down again. Close, dry, detailed, in a quiet room.
cameras16880431Sharp mechanical camera shutter clicks and clacks, film advance ratchet winding, a flashbulb pop, spaced out with silence between them, recorded in a large reverberant room. Dry transient clicks, no continuous tone.
steps12440108A heavy wooden chair scraping back on a parquet floor, then several slow leather-soled footsteps on parquet in a large echoing room. Close and dry.

Negative prompt on all four: music, melody, singing, instruments, rhythm, beat, speech, dialogue, narration. Bed and cameras are second takes — the prompt was rewritten, the seed changed with it.

Discussion
  1. Nobody captions a room tone. Should they? What would a sound credit look like on a thirty-second video, and would anyone read it?
  2. The narration in the reel is a recorded human voice. If it were synthetic, would you disclose that? Does the answer change for the crowd murmur?
  3. Stable Audio Open is trained largely on Freesound's CC0 library — sounds people recorded and gave away. Whose coughs are in that 1945 hall?

3 — upscale

S16–S18 · 15 min

Same 1000×800 patch from each — left: lanczos enlargement · right: Remacri

One frame cut down the middle — left: lanczos · right: Remacri

Model
4x_foolhardy_Remacri, ESRGAN architecture
Where
ComfyUI local, RTX 3090 Ti, --disable-pinned-memory --cache-none
Graph
VHS_LoadVideo → UpscaleModelLoader → ImageUpscaleWithModel → ImageScaleBy → SaveImage, 8 frames per submission
Generation time
3.66 s per frame · 901 frames · ≈55 min total
Done to the archive
the model runs at its trained 4× (4272×3200), then one lanczos reduction to 2× (2136×1600). Edges are reconstructed; film grain is removed as noise
Baseline
the same frames enlarged with lanczos only, which adds no detail

Detail added, measured on one face at the delivered 2×

ModelDetailOver baseline
lanczos (baseline)2.27—
4x_foolhardy_Remacri2.34+3 %
4xlsdirplus_v12.35+4 %
4x-UltraSharp2.51+11 %
4x_NMKD-Siax_200k3.19+41 %

Faces in the source are about twenty pixels tall. Remacri is not available on Comfy Cloud; 4x-UltraSharp and 4x_NMKD-Siax are.

Discussion
  1. The grain is part of the recording. Is removing it restoration, or retouching?
  2. Your platform offers two upscalers and both add grain to skin that has none. You cannot install the careful one. Do you use what is there, or not upscale at all?
  3. Legibility against invention: at native size and speed nobody watches. Is that worth it? The reel above answered no.

4 — colour

S19–S21 · 15 min

The same seconds of the signing, three times.

1 — original

2 — colour laid over the original

3 — each frame re-invented by the model

Model
Qwen-Image-Edit fp8 (Apache-2.0), with qwen_2.5_vl_7b_fp8_scaled and qwen_image_vae
Where
ComfyUI local, RTX 3090 Ti, one frame per submission
Sampler
20 steps, cfg 2.5, euler, simple, denoise 1.0, ModelSamplingAuraFlow shift 3.0, at 1068×800
Generation time
117–156 s per frame, warm
Playback
10 fps, 6 s. All three clips are the same span of film
Clip 1
the source frames, unmodified
Clip 3
the model's output as returned. Denoise 1.0 is required — at 0.6, 0.4 and 0.25 the frame survives but almost no colour arrives
Clip 2
the source frame's L channel kept, a and b taken from the generation and fitted to the source luminance with a guided filter (radius 12, eps 1e-3). Shapes, edges and grain come from the film; only chroma comes from the model
Colourisation prompt
names olive-brown tunics, gold shoulder boards, red collar tabs, field grey, dark panelling, tungsten light — a historical claim typed by the operator

Frame-to-frame variation, against the film's own noise floor

RegionVariation
tunic4×
face4.6×
blank plaster wall17.6×

Nothing in the pipeline enforces consistency between frames; each frame is generated independently. There is no colourisation model on Comfy Cloud — clip 2 is produced by tools/module03_colorize.py, outside ComfyUI.

Discussion
  1. The model returned a plausible modern photograph where a document had been, and reported success. Nothing failed. What in your process would have caught it? Name the step.
  2. Keeping the film's luminance means no shape can move. Does that make the colour honest, or only make the invention harder to see?
  3. The blank wall varies 17.6× the film's own noise. Is that a defect to fix, or the only visible sign the viewer gets that this is generated?

Homework

S23

Start a reference board on Are.na

are.na — make an account and open a channel for your own film. Put references in it as you find them: images, frames, typography, colour, layouts, anything that answers the question the moodboard block asked — how should this look.

A channel is a working surface, not a presentation. It is allowed to be unfinished, contradictory, and full of things you will throw away. Bring the link to module 04 on 25.08.
