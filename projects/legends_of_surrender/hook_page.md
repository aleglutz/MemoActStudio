## 1. What this sheet is for

Two dates carry the end of the war in Europe: the 8th of May and the 9th of May. Both are
official, both are correct, and the distance between them is one delayed signature in a
Wehrmacht engineers' school in Berlin-Karlshorst.

The numbers below are the only ones this reel is about. The one in the corner was written
by whoever filed the sheet, and means nothing at all.

## 2. The two dates

**7 May 1945, Reims.** Colonel General Jodl signs the unconditional surrender of the
Wehrmacht. The guns are to fall silent at 23:01 on the 8th.

**8/9 May 1945, Karlshorst.** The High Command signs again, at about a quarter to one in
the morning. In Moscow it is already the 9th, and it stays the 9th.
